01

Representation learning · MathNLP 2026

Learning maps from question text

Finding alternative ways to organize educational content.

What if the text of a question could help define the concepts used to model learning? I clustered question embeddings into alternative educational taxonomies and tested them in a knowledge-tracing model.

The best observed settings produced relative DKT AUC gains of 6.4% and 3.8% on two datasets. Those settings were identified using test results, so they are best-observed comparisons rather than independently selected estimates. The work was accepted at MathNLP at EMNLP 2026.

Multi-agent AI · EDM 2025

Math, made by a team of agents

Language models that write, critique, and refine math problems.

I developed collaborative workflows in which language-model agents generate and review mathematics problems, with attention to the topic and requested difficulty.

The strongest workflow improved difficulty matching by 12.5% and overall quality by 5.2%, relative to the best baseline for each metric. These were automated evaluation results, not measurements of student learning.

Interpretability · EDM 2025

Inside a student model

Tracing the relationships a model learns between concepts.

I investigated the structure learned by Deep Knowledge Tracing, a model that predicts student responses, and found relationships resembling educational prerequisites.

The work extracts directed graphs from learned representations and compares graph-derived exercise groups with matched random groups. The results help interpret predictive behavior; they do not establish causal effects on learning.

04

NLP · Student modeling

When meaning isn't the whole story

Testing whether semantic similarity explains learned concept relations.

I built text-derived representations of educational concepts and compared them with the relationships a student model learns from response sequences.

Semantic similarity showed only weak alignment with the learned structure, and semantic initialization did not reliably improve full-data prediction. The study includes embedding pipelines, model variants, and reduced-data experiments. The manuscript is prepared for submission to JEDM.

From wireless theory to hardware

Testing interference alignment on a real wireless testbed.

I prototyped interference-alignment, synchronization, and channel-estimation methods in MATLAB and on wireless hardware during my earlier UCLA research.

The work demonstrated approximately 30–40 dB SINR gains per link and contributed to an IEEE MILCOM paper on low-overhead frequency-offset estimation.

Research reproduction · Satellite communications

Deep learning for beam hopping

Rebuilding a published approach to allocating satellite resources.

A local reproduction of a published beam-hopping pipeline, connecting optimization-generated labels, a neural network, and schedule-quality analysis.

The reported local runs use public traffic samples and a synthetic channel substitute because the original antenna and link-budget files were unavailable. The project includes diagnostic experiments and reproducible reports; it is not an exact reproduction of the original study's conditions.

12

Education · Prototype

AutoMATH

Paper homework that adapts to the student.

A teacher reviews a class dashboard, looks at what each student is finding difficult, and chooses what goes into the next homework packet. The output is a printable PDF; the teacher stays in charge of the assignment.

The local prototype connects a California Grade 7 curriculum, a question bank, knowledge tracing, answer ingestion, and an editor for accepting, regenerating, or removing suggested homework blocks.
