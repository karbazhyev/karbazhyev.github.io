Kia Karbasi

AI & NLP Researcher

Los Angeles, CA

Hi! I’m Kia, an AI and NLP researcher with a PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from UCLA. My research focuses on multi-agent language-model systems, text representations, and understanding how models learn.

At UCLA, I worked with Gregory J. Pottie on AI for intelligent tutoring systems: generating math problems with teams of language-model agents, learning educational taxonomies from question text, and interpreting the relationships learned by student models. You can explore my research and projects or read my dissertation.

I’m also a Senior Member of Technical Staff at The Aerospace Corporation, where I work on satellite-network simulation and analysis. Previously, I built workflow automation at Visa and researched wireless communications at UCLA. I hold an MS from UCLA and a BS from the University of Maryland. Here’s my resume.

Outside of work, I enjoy filmmaking, photography, literature and poetry, philosophy, playing guitar, and travel.

I’d love to hear from you! Email is the best way to reach me.
